# GradCapital video script (about 2:30)

## 1. Hook (0:00-0:20) | On screen: your face

Hi, I'm Utkarsh, a fourth-year student at IIT Dharwad. Right now, somewhere, a factory is making a bad part, and nobody knows it yet. By the time they find out, the material, the machine time and the money are already gone.

## 2. Why me (0:20-0:50) | On screen: your face

I saw this world up close as an AI intern at Rossell Techsys and Moog. And one thing kept bothering me. A camera can tell you a part is bad, but not why. The machine knows exactly how that part was made, but never finds out which parts failed. Two systems, side by side, never talking. So I started building the bridge.

## 3. What it is (0:50-1:10) | On screen: your face

That's Qualyxus. It inspects every part and traces each defect back to the machine cycle that made it, so the factory can fix the cause instead of sorting scrap. And it runs fully offline, because many defence and aerospace plants can't send images to the cloud.

## 4. Demo (1:10-1:40) | On screen: the dashboard

Here it is, running live on simulated parts. Six cameras check every part, and it passes only if all six views pass. This one failed, and here's the exact view that caught it. Here's the health of the machine that made it. And every verdict goes into a tamper-evident log.

## 5. Hardware (1:40-1:52) | On screen: the rotating 3D model

And this is my design for the station that will see every face of a real part.

## 6. Honest proof (1:52-2:12) | On screen: your face

Today it works on a public benchmark and in simulation. It hasn't touched a real factory part yet. I've spoken with the heads at Rossell Techsys, Rotary Connectors and Moog about testing it, and Rotary Connectors has agreed to help build this inspector.

## 7. Close (2:12-2:30) | On screen: your face

Your grant gets me the camera, the lighting and the edge computer to cross that line, from simulation to real parts. Factories shouldn't find out after it's too late. I'm building Qualyxus so they don't have to. Thank you.
